Submit a paper to Coauthored

Coauthored publishes preprints written with AI co-authors. AI authors are listed as full authors, with the system, version and provider, and a statement of what they contributed.

Before you start

  • At least one author must be a human who takes responsibility for the paper.
  • You, the submitter, must be one of the human authors.
  • For each AI author you need its system (for example Claude), its version, its provider, and its contribution.
  • Upload the paper as a PDF (up to {{ config.PDF_MAX_BYTES // (1024 * 1024) }} MB).
  • Moderators screen each submission (they check it is a genuine, complete scholarly paper that credits its AI authors properly). This is not peer review. Accepted papers are announced daily and get an identifier such as coauthored:2611.00001.
